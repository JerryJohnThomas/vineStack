Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to run VineStack, we never sell personal data, and an organisation’s member data belongs to that organisation — we look after it for them.

1. Who this covers

This policy explains how VineStack (“we”, “us”) handles personal data when you visit vinestack.app, sign up for the Service, or use a site that runs on VineStack.

  • Our customers — the organisations that use VineStack and the people who manage their sites. For this data we are the controller.
  • Members and visitors of an organisation’s site — for example people who join a course, register for an event, give a donation or send an enquiry. That organisation is the controller and we process the data on its behalf. Questions about it are best sent to the organisation first.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and profile picture from your Google sign-in.
  • What you put into the Service: your organisation’s name, website content, images, settings, and member information you add or collect.
  • Enquiries and demo requests: what you write to us, such as your name, email, organisation and message.
  • Billing: your plan, invoices and payment status. Card or bank details are handled by our payment partners; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage and technical data: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address, and error logs — used to keep the Service secure and working.
  • Cookies and local storage: to keep you signed in and remember preferences. Analytics (Google Analytics) is used on vinestack.app, and on an organisation’s site only if that organisation switches it on.

3. How we use it

  • to provide, run and secure the Service, and to fix problems;
  • to create and manage your account and site;
  • to bill you for paid plans;
  • to reply to your questions and send you messages about the Service (for example a new site being published, or a change to these policies);
  • to prevent fraud and abuse, such as sites that impersonate others;
  • to understand, in aggregate, how the Service is used so we can improve it.

We use your data on the basis of our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running a safe and reliable service, your consent where we ask for it, and legal obligations. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use members’ data for our own marketing.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • hosting, database and storage — Cloudflare, Supabase and Render;
  • email delivery — such as Brevo and Amazon Web Services;
  • sign-in — Google;
  • payments — our billing partner for VineStack plans, and each organisation’s own Razorpay or Stripe account for the payments it collects;
  • Claude by Anthropic — only when an organisation switches on the Claude connection, and only the information needed for the task asked.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people from harm, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

5. Where data is stored

Data is stored with our providers in data centres that may be outside your country, including in India, Singapore, the European Union and the United States. Where data leaves your country, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • account and site data — for as long as your organisation uses VineStack, then up to 30 days so you can ask for an export;
  • unpublished previews — paused after 7 days and removed after a further 30;
  • billing records — as long as tax law requires;
  • technical logs — for a limited time, normally no more than 12 months.

7. Security

Each organisation’s data is kept separate from every other’s, connections are encrypted, payment secrets are stored encrypted, and the Service is backed up regularly. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the EU/UK GDPR — you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict some uses, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, write to hello@vinestack.app. If your data was collected by an organisation on VineStack, we will pass your request to that organisation or help it respond.

9. Children

VineStack accounts are for adults. Organisations that work with children are responsible for getting any parental consent the law requires before adding a child’s information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will tell administrators before they take effect.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: hello@vinestack.app.
