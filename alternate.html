Questions

Who is VineStack for?

Institutes, academies and Bible schools first — and any organisation that gathers people: churches, non-profits, training providers and small businesses. Start with a website; add the rest when you need it.

Does the money go through VineStack?

No. Fees and donations go straight into your organisation’s own Razorpay or Stripe account. VineStack never holds your money.

Can we use our own domain and branding?

Yes, on every plan — your domain, your colours, your logo and your words, all changed from your own admin.

What does “works with Claude” mean?

Connect Claude and run everyday work by asking — check who owes fees, approve sign-ups, grade the next submission, publish an article. Every change is confirmed before it happens.

Can you move us from our current tools?

Yes. Setup is included on yearly plans, and on Pro we migrate your data for you.
