Refund policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: if VineStack isn’t right for you, ask within 14 days of your first payment and we’ll refund it in full. You can cancel at any time after that and keep your plan until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

1. New subscriptions — 14-day money-back

If you are not happy with a paid plan, contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund that payment in full, monthly or yearly. No complicated questions — though we’d love to hear what didn’t work.

2. Renewals

  • You can cancel at any time from your admin or by writing to us. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again.
  • We don’t refund partly used periods after a renewal, except for yearly plans: if a yearly plan renews and you tell us within 14 days of the renewal that you didn’t mean to continue, we will refund that renewal.
  • If you move to a cheaper plan, the change applies from your next renewal.

3. Setup fee

The one-time setup fee is refundable in full until we start setting up your site. Once setup work has begun, it is refundable only if we have not delivered what we agreed.

4. When we’re at fault

If the Service is unavailable for a long period, or a paid feature doesn’t work as described and we can’t fix it, we will offer a fair refund or credit for the affected time, whatever the dates above say.

5. Payments to organisations on VineStack

Donations, course fees and event payments made to an organisation that uses VineStack go straight into that organisation’s own account — VineStack never receives that money. Refunds for those payments are decided by the organisation; please contact them directly. If you can’t reach them, write to us and we will help put you in touch.

6. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@vinestack.app from the address you signed up with, with your organisation’s name and site address. We aim to reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and normally arrive within 5–10 working days, depending on your bank. If your payment was handled by our payment partner as merchant of record, the refund is issued through them.

7. Your legal rights

This policy doesn’t affect any rights you have under the consumer law of your country.
